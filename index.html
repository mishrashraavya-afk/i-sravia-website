About I-SRAVIA

Technology should make room for more ways to communicate.

I-SRAVIA stands for Indian Sign-Language Responsive & Voice Intelligent Assistant. We are an accessibility technology company exploring sign-language recognition, speech technology and tools that could make everyday communication easier.

Sign languages have their own grammar and culture. Technology can support communication, but meaningful progress depends on listening to signers, educators and the communities the work is intended to serve.

Our journey

From one school visit to a growing research venture.

Every milestone has moved I-SRAVIA closer to a more accessible, software-driven approach to communication.

  1. 22 April 2022

    Where it began

    A visit to Shree Harsha School for the Deaf, Jatni sparked the idea behind I-SRAVIA and its goal of making communication more accessible.

  2. November 2022

    The first prototype

    Developed and submitted the first I-SRAVIA prototype to the DST-INSPIRE MANAK programme.

  3. 2023–2024

    A major transformation

    Evolved I-SRAVIA from an initial hardware-based concept into a software-driven solution, expanding its potential for accessibility and real-world use.

  4. 19 September 2024

    National recognition

    Received recognition through the DST-INSPIRE MANAK National Award, marking a major milestone in the project's journey.

  5. 9 December 2024

    Funding the next stage

    Secured product funding through the Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) fund to support further prototype development.

  6. 6 February 2025

    Taking the idea further

    Pitched I-SRAVIA to reviewers at STPI, continuing the work toward developing and validating the project.

  7. 2025

    ₹44,000 raised for development

    Secured a total of ₹44,000 in initial funding from the Department of Science and Technology (DST) and KVS to support the project's development.

  8. November 2025

    Research publication and presentation

    Published peer-reviewed research and presented the work at the 5th Indian International Conference on Industrial Engineering and Operations Management in Vellore.

  9. 2026

    Selected for Sakura Science

    Selected for the Sakura Science Exchange Programme, with an opportunity to participate in an international science exchange in Japan.

  10. Today

    Still evolving

    I-SRAVIA has grown from a school visit and an early idea into a funded, peer-reviewed prototype with a developing business model. Community engagement and further validation are underway with ISL signers and reviewers.
