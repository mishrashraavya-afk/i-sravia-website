Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

I-SRAVIA (“we,” “our,” “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy and security of the people who engage with our work. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you interact with our website, pilot programme, research activities, outreach, or communications.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide Directly

  • Name, email address, and contact information when you sign up for a pilot programme, volunteer, or contact the team.
  • Feedback, comments, questions, or other information you submit through forms or email.
  • Details you provide in connection with research participation, collaborations, or support requests.

b. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information such as browser type, operating system, and device model.
  • Usage data, including pages visited, time spent on our site, and interactions with the platform.
  • IP address and general location information (for example, country or city) for analytics, security, and service improvement.
  • We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this website. It sets cookies and receives anonymised usage data. We have IP anonymisation enabled and do not use Google Analytics for advertising. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

c. Data Processed by the I-SRAVIA Application

  • Sign language input: Video data captured through your device camera for sign language recognition.
  • Speech input: Audio data captured for speech-to-text conversion.
  • Note: Video and audio are generally processed in real time. Unless you explicitly consent, we do not routinely store this data on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our sign language recognition and communication support tools.
  • To personalise and improve your experience with the platform and project updates.
  • To respond to inquiries, support requests, and partnership conversations.
  • To conduct research, improve recognition models, and evaluate accessibility outcomes.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse, and support responsible community engagement.

3. Data Storage & Retention

  • By default, real-time video and audio data are not stored for general website use.
  • If you participate in research or contribute training data, storage will occur only with your informed, explicit consent.
  • Contact and account information is retained only as long as needed to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share anonymised or aggregated information with trusted research collaborators, accessibility partners, or service providers who help us host or analyse our work, subject to confidentiality obligations.

  • Research collaborators engaged in accessibility or language technology work.
  • Accessibility advocacy groups or community partners where appropriate and consensual.
  • Service providers that assist with hosting, analytics, or support, bound by confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Rights

  • Request access to the information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data, where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent for data collection or processing where consent is the lawful basis.
  • File a complaint with the relevant data protection authority in your jurisdiction.

To exercise your rights, email us at project.isravia@gmail.com.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures, including secure systems, access controls, and confidentiality practices, to protect your information. However, no system is completely infallible, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for general audiences. Users under the age of 13 should only use I-SRAVIA with appropriate parental or guardian supervision and consent where required.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and we encourage you to review it periodically.

9. Contact Us
